How to use:

  • Click on the circle to add beats
  • Drag beats to reposition them
  • Drag outside the circle to remove beats
  • Hold Ctrl while dragging to quantize to 2 divisions
  • Hold Shift while dragging to quantize to 4 divisions
  • Hold Ctrl+Shift for fine control
  • Adjust BPM with the slider to change tempo
